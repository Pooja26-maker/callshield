
# Sample 03: Lost Phone Guidance

Type: Illustrative sample
Source: Original sample content, not copied from a government website
Language: English

Q: What should someone do if their mobile phone is lost?

A: Contact the mobile operator to ask about blocking the SIM, secure important accounts, and follow the official police-reporting process applicable to their location.

Q: Where can a citizen find official guidance on blocking a lost phone in India?

A: Consult the official Sanchar Saathi portal and follow its current instructions for lost or stolen mobile devices.
