# 10 real questions my user will ask

# Citizen Help Finder — Evaluation Questions

These questions will be used to evaluate whether the application can help citizens find relevant telecom consumer information from the collected documents.

1. What steps can a telecom consumer take to register a complaint?
2. What is the role of the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) in consumer protection?
3. How can a consumer find information about unsolicited commercial communications?
4. What information should a consumer keep when raising a telecom complaint?
5. What is the purpose of mobile number portability?
6. Where can a consumer look for answers to common telecom-related questions?
7. What consumer protection measures are described in the collected TRAI documents?
8. What is the grievance redressal process described in the available documents?
9. Which collected document contains information about consumer protection regulations?
10. If the available documents do not contain an answer, how should the application respond?

## Evaluation notes

- Verify answers against the collected source documents.
- Record the source document and page number wherever available.
- Do not invent answers when the documents do not provide sufficient evidence.
- Review these questions again after the document retrieval and citation features are implemented.
